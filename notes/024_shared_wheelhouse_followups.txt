Shared wheelhouse follow-ups

Image builds and acquisition pin wheelhouse-tool 0.6.5. Image input labels
remain tied to the bytes actually admitted at build time.
When a cold wheel selection fails, the conflict check hashes compatible cached
candidates again, including during the exclusive peer-winner recheck. Large
partial pools can therefore delay readers during local verification, though the
exclusive lease is released before the shared publisher downloads artifacts.

The download launcher checks a complete pool without selecting a network route.
It admits an owned offline worker's missing-input result only after confirming
its stopped state and removal, then allows one acquisition and rechecks offline.
A separately missing wheel can coexist with a pip failure for another reason;
the preflight does not claim that the missing wheel caused that failure.

The abnormal Docker client-disconnection boundary still needs owner-native
proof of target worker retirement. Preserve resources and evidence when that
retirement is uncertain.

The operator-input lane remains incomplete after removing its retired fake
wheel-capture branch. A focused run has three failing cases and one error:
two wheelhouse cases lack the acquirer input in the fixture; the checker case
models host UID 1000 but its temporary directories are owned by mapped UID 0;
the interactive case exits before the interface prompt and its PTY reports
EIO, with the earlier cause not yet established. Do not fake filesystem
ownership or relax the production owner check to make these fixtures pass.
Other older tests still expect removed wheel-capture receipts or invoke the
retired private docker.sh run operation; reconcile them with the shared owner.

The older full checker launcher fixture predates streamed contexts and mapped
identities. Its complete cancellation and cleanup suite needs reconciliation;
focused launcher witnesses cover the shared-pool cutover, but are not a claim
that the entire older suite passes.
