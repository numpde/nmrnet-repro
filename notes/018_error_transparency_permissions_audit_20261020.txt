## Reproduced error losses

1. process_reports.py::failure_evidence omits ExchangeFailed.response_head and
   ChatFailed.reason. A received HTTP 429 plus x-request-id followed by timeout
   renders only ChatFailed/ExchangeFailed/TimeoutError, losing both received facts.
   Requested fix: reviewed status/correlation projection; no raw header/body dump.
2. PreparationRetirementFailed(operation_failure=InputRejected(message,
   diagnostic=...), cleanup=EIO) renders the message and EIO, but drops the native
   reader diagnostic. Cleanup prevents ordinary AnalysisFailure persistence.
3. Two ToolConversation endpoints failing request-limit then HTTP 503 leave two
   entries in endpoint_failures, but the propagated exception report contains only
   the second cause. Successful fallback retains reason/status in public routes,
   not the earlier protected cause. Preserve safe endpoint evidence through an
   existing boundary, without transcripts or a new journal.
4. Reviewed AllocationUnconfirmed, RetirementUnconfirmed and ExecutionRefused
   render as bare class names. Their safe absent/malformed/unconfirmed-receipt
   reasons disappear. Further source review identifies ResultRejected and other
   binding outcome classes missing from the explicit projection inventory.
5. process.py::_failure_report writes stderr before trying _publish. A synthetic
   BrokenPipeError at stderr prevents failure.json publication even when the file
   sink is available. The independent outputs must both be attempted.
6. The service proxy projects DNS owner-created OSError reasons with errno=None
   as just OSError; errno=None, losing NXDOMAIN rcode, truncated reply, and
   mismatched-question distinctions. Preserve reviewed protocol reasons at owner.
