7. Controller comments after removal of direct publication (U17/U22).
   Worktree after 376c97a, reviewed 24 October at 20:35 UTC:
   packages/provider-core/src/provider_core/controller.py::operation still
   describes a publication owner, and ::_retire_session still says publication

8. Waiting for a temporarily unavailable relay during idle admission (U27/A11).
   Worktree after 376c97a, reviewed 24 October at 20:40 UTC:
   nmrnet_provider/admission.py::require_admission_ready calls delivery.pending,
   while service.py::_until_api_available currently retries only ApiFailure.
   A local DeliveryUnavailable during this read can end the producer run rather
   than wait in process. Docker restart supplies the present recovery path.
   Consider paced, stop-responsive local read recovery later, preserving the
   original transport owner. The executor already retries its unchanged result
   handoff; this entry concerns idle admission, not an accepted result's delivery.

9. Read-only deployment command receipts (U13/U17/U23/U27, A05/A11).
   New tools/delivery_deployment.sh, worktree after 376c97a, reviewed 24 October
   at 20:57 UTC: status/logs use a persistent query receipt directory and refuse
   another query if its previous complete marker is absent. These are read-only
   operations. Later, narrow this mechanism so a failed diagnostic read does not
   require manual receipt reconciliation before another status/logs call, and
   minimize retained copies of Docker-owned logs. Surface normal stderr now;
   simplifying the receipt policy is deferred, without a new recovery framework.
